What happens next

The server validates the archive against the results schema, superposes the predicted complex on the reference's pocket Ca atoms, runs PLIP on the pose, the relaxed complex and the crystal, classifies the binding mode, reads the trajectory summary and grades the run. It takes seconds, not minutes, and the answer arrives in this page.
